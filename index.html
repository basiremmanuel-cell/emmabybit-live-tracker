How the signal works

The tracker screens Bybit Spot USDT pairs whose names match a maintained meme-coin keyword list, then ranks candidates using 24-hour price movement and quote volume. BUY WATCH requires positive momentum and sufficient relative volume; SELL / EXIT WATCH flags sharp negative movement or a weakening candidate. Refreshing every 10 seconds updates the live ticker data. The trend sparkline is a compact visual of recent 24-hour change context, not a candlestick forecast.

Data source: Bybit public market ticker endpoint. This page does not place orders, connect to your account, store credentials, or access your wallet.
